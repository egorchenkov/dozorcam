Your cameras, your Telegram, no cloud

Dozorcam watches IP cameras on your own Linux box, recognises people — not leaves, shadows or the cat — and sends a snapshot with the person boxed and a clip of the moment to your Telegram group. Each camera gets its own topic; the whole system is run from the chat.

Install in 3 steps Source on GitHub
  • Free and open source
  • No cloud, no account
  • Hikvision, ONVIF, any RTSP
  • amd64 and arm64

Who it is for

For people who already have IP cameras and want to know when someone actually walks in — without a vendor cloud, a monthly fee or yet another app.

🏡

House or summer cottage

A few cameras at the gate, the door and the yard. You get a message when a person shows up — and not every time the wind moves a branch.

🛠

Workshop, garage, small shop

The family or the team share one Telegram group: everyone sees events, anyone can ask a camera for a fresh frame or a clip.

🖥

Home server enthusiasts

One Docker Compose file on a mini PC, a home server or an ARM board. Your video never leaves your network and your chat.

What it does

Everything a small camera setup needs, and nothing that sends your yard to someone else's server.

👤

Detects people, not motion

A cheap motion gate with a per-camera noise floor, then a neural person detector (YOLOX-Tiny by default, YOLOv5/YOLOv8 optional). The threshold is calibrated per camera against the scene's own noise.

🤝

Confirmed by the camera

Cameras with their own person detection (Hikvision over ONVIF) confirm a person: when the camera says “someone is here”, Dozorcam looks closer and does not miss a small or half-hidden figure.

🪣

Ignores things that never move

A bag, a bucket or a bush that the model mistakes for a person is checked against the same spot seconds earlier — if nothing moved, it is not an event.

📸

Snapshots and clips in Telegram

A snapshot with the person boxed arrives in the camera's topic. One tap builds a clip around that moment from the recording buffer — playable right in the chat.

🎛

Control from the chat

The “Control” topic shows every camera's state and storage; buttons pause a camera, rename it, switch the detector model and set thresholds.

🔎

Add cameras in a minute

/add finds cameras on the network (ONVIF, WS-Discovery, RTSP), asks for the login and deletes that message. A brand-new Hikvision is even activated for you.

🔒

No cloud — your data stays yours

Video is a short rolling buffer in RAM on your node; the long-term copy is your own Telegram chat. No account, no telemetry, no subscription.

🩺

Explains its decisions

A diagnostic journal records every rejected candidate and why, and a daily summary flags possible misses — so “why didn't it tell me?” has an answer.

📦

Small and locked down

One multi-arch image for engine and bot: non-root, read-only root filesystem, no capabilities, healthchecks, optional mutual TLS between the parts.

How a detection happens

Each step is cheap until the next one is needed, so a small machine handles several cameras.

  1. 1 · BufferRecording from each camera into a rolling RAM buffer — no disk wear.
  2. 2 · Motion gateOnly frames that changed above the scene's noise go further.
  3. 3 · Person detectorYOLO scores people; a person must hold for consecutive frames.
  4. 4 · ChecksStill-object filter; the camera's own person signal confirms or vetoes.
  5. 5 · TelegramSnapshot with a box in the camera topic; clip on a tap.

New in 0.1.3: one language throughout

Everything you read is in the language you chose, English or Russian: the bot, camera search and add errors, the daily summary, the health check. Set it once for the whole installation with CCTV_LANG or per chat with /lang.

Fewer misses and false alarms

Confirm mode: the camera's person signal opens a window where one frame is enough. A camera can trust its own silence, so an IR-lit bush at dawn no longer becomes a person.

Diagnostics

Journal of rejected candidates with the reason and a daily summary that pairs cameras watching the same spot. Full changelog

Install in 3 steps

You need a Linux machine (amd64 or arm64) with Docker and the compose plugin that can reach api.telegram.org, and IP cameras on the same network.

  1. Create a Telegram bot

    Open @BotFather, send /newbot and copy the token.

  2. Start Dozorcam

    git clone https://github.com/egorchenkov/dozorcam.git && cd dozorcam
cp .env.example .env && sed -i 's/^CCTV_BOT_TOKEN=.*/CCTV_BOT_TOKEN=<token>/' .env
docker compose pull --ignore-pull-failures && docker compose up -d
    The image ghcr.io/egorchenkov/dozorcam is pulled for your architecture; to pin this release put CCTV_IMAGE_TAG=0.1.3 into .env, and CCTV_LANG=ru for Russian everywhere. If the registry is unreachable, compose builds the image locally.

  3. Claim the bot and add cameras

    docker compose logs bot | grep SETUP prints a one-time code — send /start <code> to the bot in a private chat and you are the owner. Create a group with Topics turned on, add the bot as an admin (“Manage topics”, “Delete messages”) — it creates the “Control” topic. Send /add there.

How to use it

Everything happens in one Telegram group with Topics. Commands for the bot:

CommandWhat it does
/start <code>Once, in a private chat: makes you the owner of the bot.
/setupFinishes the group setup and tells you which admin right is missing.
/addSearches the network for cameras; or /add rtsp://host:port/path for a camera outside the search.
/menuLists cameras and links to their topics.
/modelSwitches the person detector model on the fly, with automatic rollback.
/lang en|ruInterface language. Default: CCTV_LANG of the installation, else your Telegram language, else English.

Camera topics

Events arrive here. The pinned panel shows the stream and detector state; buttons: 📷 Frame, 🎞 Clip 30 s, 🔄 Status, 🔔 motion notifications, ⏸ Pause, ✏️ Name, ⚙️ Settings, 🗑 Retire.

Clips

Under every event — “🎞 Clip around this frame”: the clip is cut from the buffer around the moment the person was seen, even if the detector was a few seconds behind.

“Control” topic

State of all cameras and storage, ➕ Add camera, 🧠 Detector model, 🎯 Thresholds (auto-calibration or manual per camera). A new camera starts with person detection off: turn it on in ⚙️ Settings after a look at the scene.

Supported cameras

KindHow it is connected
HikvisionFound by the search; built-in RTSP and snapshot templates where ONVIF is silent. A new camera is activated from /add (password set, ONVIF user created). The camera's own person detection can gate or confirm Dozorcam's detector.
ONVIF (Profile S)Found automatically (local /24 networks and WS-Discovery); stream and snapshot addresses come from ONVIF.
Any RTSP camera/add rtsp://host:port/path, optionally a second low-resolution stream for the detector; the snapshot is taken from the stream.

Tested live on Hikvision G0/G2/G5 and Tantos cameras. Other vendors should be activated with their own tools first; they should work over ONVIF/RTSP but are untested.

Honest limits

  • No on-disk archive: the buffer holds minutes in RAM; the Telegram chat is the long-term copy.
  • Timestamps in captions are Moscow time (MSK) for now; the time zone is not configurable yet.
  • The node must reach api.telegram.org.
  • Interface languages: English and Russian.
